My experience spans pharmaceutical data science, clinical statistics, and biomedical research. At Pfizer, I worked on real-world patient retention modeling, pharmaceutical sales forecasting, and automated analytical reporting using Python, survival analysis, machine learning, and Dataiku.

Projects

Patient Retention Modeling & Pharmaceutical Forecasting — Pfizer
