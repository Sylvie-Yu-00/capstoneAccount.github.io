Patient Retention Modeling & Pharmaceutical Forecasting

Pfizer · Data Science Internship

Applied statistical and machine-learning methods to real-world pharmaceutical data, with projects spanning patient retention, forecasting, and automated analytical reporting.

  • Modeled patient retention and drop-off using Kaplan–Meier methods, Cox proportional hazards, accelerated failure time models, and LightGBM to identify consistent risk signals.
  • Improved a pharmaceutical forecasting workflow through anomaly adjustment, feature engineering, model comparison, and post-processing, reducing average monthly forecast error from 17% to 3% at the franchise level in project evaluations.
  • Built reusable Python workflows and LLM-assisted HTML reporting processes in Dataiku to standardize analysis, validation, and project handover.

Lipoprotein & Lipidomic Profiling in Acute Myocardial Infarction

Undergraduate Thesis

Investigated lipid-metabolism patterns associated with acute myocardial infarction by integrating clinical variables, lipoprotein subfractions, and lipidomic measurements.
